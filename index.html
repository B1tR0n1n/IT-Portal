Build secrets

The standard build password, the security answer, and the local admin account name are not written into these pages. They ship encrypted and decrypt in your browser only after you enter the passphrase, for that tab alone. Unlock once and the checklist fills itself in; leave it locked and every copy says ask IT instead.
