Vault builder

Encrypts the build secrets into the file the portal reads. Everything happens in this browser — the values below are never transmitted, and this page has no network calls at all.

Choose a passphrase you would defend in front of an auditor. The published ciphertext can be downloaded by anyone who finds the URL and attacked offline at their leisure, so its strength is entirely the strength of what you type. A long phrase of unrelated words beats a short complicated one.

Entropy estimate — waiting

Replace assets/vault.json with this and push. To rotate the password later, run this page again and replace the file — nothing else changes.

What this protects, and what it doesn't

AES-GCM with a 256-bit key, derived from your passphrase with PBKDF2-SHA256 at 310,000 iterations against a random salt. Without the passphrase the ciphertext is meaningless, so the credentials are genuinely absent from the published site.

It does not protect against a weak passphrase, a tech pasting the passphrase into a chat, or someone reading the decrypted values over a shoulder. It also does not make the site private — every page and asset is still world-readable. Treat this as keeping a password off a public page, not as access control.
